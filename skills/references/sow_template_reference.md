### Services Approach

Airia will employ a "train the trainer" approach to equip Customer's team with the necessary skills and knowledge to effectively utilize the in-scope Airia Services. This approach ensures Customer's team becomes self-sufficient and capable of sustaining the implemented solutions post-engagement. Airia will support Customer with product guidance, recommendations, and Software best practices to support the deployment of the Airia Services. Customer is responsible for the configuration of their Agents unless otherwise agreed upon.

## 4. Delivery Methodology

Depending on the Airia Professional Services Offering listed on an Order Form, the delivery of Airia Services will generally follow the below methodology to the extent applicable to ensure successful implementation and roll out of the Airia Software across Customer's organization.

Phase 1: Design — Project Initiation & Solution Design
Phase 2: Training — Platform Setup & Training
Phase 3: Configuration — Configuration of platform and Agents
Phase 4: Solution Review — Conduct solution testing against desired outcomes
Phase 5: Project Review — Review outcomes and project success

### Phase 1: Design
Confirm the project scope, document desired outcomes, and plan resource requirements and the configuration approach to meet the agreed upon Customer requirements.

### Phase 2: Training
Conduct training sessions to enable Customer's team to understand and use the solutions provided. Airia provides a 1-hour overview training to familiarise Customer with the Software. Additional custom training is available if additional Professional Services are purchased.

### Phase 3: Configuration
Support Customer during configuration of the Software to align with the desired outcomes and during testing to ensure functionality of solution. Customer is responsible for Agent configurations.

### Phase 4: Desired Outcomes
Support and enable Customer to use the in-scope desired outcomes configured.

### Phase 5: Project Review
Review proof of concept outcomes against the desired outcomes to confirm alignment, confirm the transfer of knowledge with Customer, and close out the project.

## 5. Delivery of the Professional Services

Depending on the Professional Services offering in-scope, the Professional Services will be delivered in a series of workshops and to the extent applicable. All meetings are conducted remotely through video conferencing platforms unless otherwise agreed by the Parties. The Airia Professional Services team will maintain a delivery checklist and will review this with Customer at regularly agreed intervals throughout the project. Customer is responsible for providing all necessary data and resources required for the project.

## 6. Services Assumptions

6.1. Customer is responsible for ensuring the availability and quality of their data and providing access to the data which they desire to use.

6.2. Professional Services are scoped with an estimated level of effort to complete the defined scope. If scope requires an additional effort or extends beyond the planned effort, Customer may be required to purchase additional hours.

6.3. Design decisions are the responsibility of Customer and guidance from Airia is to be considered but does not guarantee adherence or compliance with regulations specific to Customer's organization.

6.4. Customer is responsible for all Third-Party Services integrations (e.g., the integration itself and the software, licenses, etc.). Airia's Professional Services team may support the configuration, but responsibility remains with Customer.

6.5. It is Customer's responsibility to ensure its team has the necessary technical skills to support the project.

6.6. Airia Agents may be able to offer multilingual support, however, the Airia Services and project Documentation will be delivered in English, unless otherwise agreed by the Parties.

6.7. Any changes to the scope or deliverables will be managed through a documented change process.

6.8. Travel is not included in the Airia Services and must be agreed upon separately. The associated travel costs are to be approved by Customer in advance. On-site travel requires 14-days' advanced notice.

6.9. The Professional Services will expire on the earlier of the following: 6 months after the date of the corresponding Order Form's Effective Date or following the completion of all in-scope Professional Services.

6.10. Customer assumes responsibility for configuration, regulatory compliance, and third-party tools as they deploy the Software.

6.11. In addition to the Workshops detailed above, the Professional Services team may also provide further training workshops, email consultation and configuration assistance, or other Professional Services mutually agreed upon in writing between Customer and Airia, as may be listed in an Order Form or SOW.
